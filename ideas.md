## Chosen Approach: Blueprint Editorial

### Design Movement
Swiss editorial systems fused with architectural drafting and modern technical publishing.

### Core Principles
1. Make every section answer a decision question: what exists, what changes, and why.
2. Use visible structure—rules, numbered phases, matrices, and diagram-like connectors—to make complexity navigable.
3. Pair dark technical surfaces with warm paper panels so the site feels authoritative but not cold.
4. Keep density intentional: high information value with generous breathing room.

### Color Philosophy
Deep blueprint navy establishes trust and infrastructure depth. Warm ivory acts as the editorial canvas, making long-form comparison content comfortable to read. Signal orange marks the preferred path and action moments; muted mint marks stable foundations. The palette should feel like a considered engineering decision, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Layout Paradigm
A long-form editorial runway with a persistent left-side section index on desktop, asymmetric hero composition, and horizontal decision bands. Cards should feel like document excerpts or pinned architecture notes, not uniform rounded tiles.

### Signature Elements
- Blueprint grid lines and coordinate labels used sparingly as structural texture.
- Orange “preferred” route markers that connect recommendations across sections.
- Small monospaced metadata labels for phases, risk levels, and decision states.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should clarify decisions. Tabs reveal comparison layers, phase navigation updates the reading position, and copy/share actions provide immediate utility. Motion is quick and mechanical—like sliding cards into a drafting system—not decorative.

### Animation
Use short 180–260ms ease-out transitions for tabs, chips, and reveals. Let the hero diagram line draw in once, then keep the page calm. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid parallax and constant movement; the content is the product.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk for confident, technical headlines. Body: Newsreader for warm editorial reading. Metadata: IBM Plex Mono for labels, phase numbers, and architecture primitives. Use tight display tracking and generous body line-height.

### Brand Essence
A shareable operating decision for turning a capable video platform into a durable content factory.

Personality: exacting, calm, forward-looking.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive and specific. CTAs are practical, never inflated. Microcopy explains the trade-off in one sentence.

Example lines:
- “Scale the foundation before you multiply the features.”
- “Keep the media engine. Replace the fragile boundaries.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact monogram built from two offset brackets forming an “S” pathway: one bracket represents the current system, the other the preferred scale route. It should work as a small blueprint stamp and favicon without text.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Orange `#F26B38` — the ownable marker for the preferred path, migration action, and high-leverage decision.

## Style Decisions
- Use the Blueprint Editorial direction consistently across the page.
- Prefer warm ivory reading surfaces over pure white.
- Use dark navy for hero/architecture sections and signal orange only for meaningful emphasis.
- Avoid generic purple gradients, excessive rounded cards, centered-only layouts, and Inter.
