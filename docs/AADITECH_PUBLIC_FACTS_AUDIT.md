## Claims excluded from the first reel

The site includes testimonial blocks with names and company labels that appear generic or placeholder-like, as well as placeholder client logos. These will not be used as endorsements, reviews, ratings, or proof points. The first reel will use only direct company-description claims from the official site and will not fabricate or reproduce customer testimonials.

The public page also contains an unresolved Google Maps API-key notice. It does not affect the factual service claims above and will not be mentioned in marketing copy.

## Content decision

A factual first marketing reel can now be drafted around the verified positioning, service themes, pan-India coverage, and the site’s stated experience/team/client figures. Claims such as “guaranteed results,” specific client outcomes, or named endorsements remain excluded unless separately supported by owner-approved evidence.
